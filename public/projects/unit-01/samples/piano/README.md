# Piano samples

**Salamander Grand Piano** by **Alexander Holm** — a Yamaha C5 grand,
recorded with two AKG C414s in an AB pair above the strings.

Licensed under the [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
Source: [sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano](https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano).

These 20 files (C2 to C7, one every three semitones, less D#5, a much softer
take than its neighbours) are the 128 kbps MP3 encodings published by the
Tone.js project
([Tonejs/audio, `salamander/`](https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/tree/master/salamander)),
copied unchanged.

Used by the Unit 01 Ear Trainer and Chord Finder through
`/projects/unit-01/apps/unit01-piano.js`, which plays the nearest recording
for each note, retuned.
